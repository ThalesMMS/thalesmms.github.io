Turing Lab support

Setup, troubleshooting, and a direct contact for help.

Start with an example

Choose a workspace and open a bundled example before building your own model.

When a simulation stops

Check the input, transition definitions and execution limits. A bounded run ending does not always prove rejection.

Opening and saving files

Choose the format supported by your workspace and read compatibility warnings before export. On the web, exported files use browser downloads.

Platform availability

The project targets Android, iOS, web, Windows, macOS and Linux. Source feature availability does not establish store availability or validation on every device.
