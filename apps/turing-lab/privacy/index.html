Turing Lab / Privacy

Turing Lab privacy policy

Effective September 5, 2026

How Turing Lab handles data, including the services and integrations you choose to use.

Local learning data

Models and simulations are processed by the application. Preferences, active sessions, teaching sessions and simulation history can be saved in local application or browser storage.

Files you choose

Import reads selected documents. Export writes or downloads the selected content; exported copies remain wherever you save them.

Network and external links

Bundled examples do not require a network connection. Help, support and project links open external websites when selected; the web version also needs its host to deliver the application.

Analytics and AI

The inspected application code does not include a cloud AI provider or an integrated analytics or crash-reporting service.

Removing local data

Clear the relevant local application or browser site data to remove stored sessions and preferences. Manage exported files separately; clearing browser storage can remove unsaved local work.

This support website

This static website does not use analytics scripts, forms, or third-party tracking scripts. GitHub Pages handles requests to the website under its own hosting and privacy practices. Visiting a source repository or contacting another service is subject to that service's policies.

Privacy questions and support

Contact Thales Matheus M Santos about Turing Lab at thalesmmsradio@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please omit patient information, private documents, and credentials.

Policy updates

This policy may change when the software's data handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version. Features and integrations can differ by release and platform.
