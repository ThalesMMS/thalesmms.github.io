Video Subtitler

Turn local videos into editable subtitles, translations and exported media.

Video Subtitler keeps video, transcript and export work together in a project. It supports on-device speech recognition and language tools, with an optional remote provider for selected text operations.

Setup & support Read the privacy policy

Editable subtitles

Import local media, transcribe speech and review subtitle text and timing.

Translation and context

Translate subtitle cues, summarize transcripts and ask questions with relevant transcript excerpts.

Dubbing

Generate speech using supported iOS voices or a downloaded OmniVoice model, with manual speaker assignment.

Export options

Export SRT subtitles or supported video formats with burned-in subtitles, sidecar files or a selectable subtitle track in MOV.
