IsiX DICOM Viewer

Browse DICOM studies, inspect images and connect to configured imaging archives.

IsiX brings a local study catalog, image navigation and measurement tools to iPhone, iPad, visionOS and Android, with Apple companion presentation workflows. Rendering, archive connections and local AI tools depend on the platform, device and installed build. The distributed macOS application is a separate Horos-derived project.

Setup & support Read the privacy policy

A study library

Import DICOM files and supported archives into a local catalog organized by study and series.

Image exploration

Adjust window and level, navigate frames and inspect metadata, with measurement, MPR and volume tools where supported.

Archive connections

Configure DIMSE or DICOMweb nodes to query, retrieve and send studies.

Platform-specific viewing

Apple builds include companion presentations and supported on-device segmentation and CT assistance. Android has its own rendering and storage stack; these features are not identical across platforms.

Platforms and separate implementations

The Swift application contains iOS/iPadOS, visionOS, watchOS and tvOS targets. Watch and TV presentation workflows differ from the main viewer. Android uses a separate Kotlin/native imaging stack.

The macOS application has its own section in this directory. A separate Windows C++/Qt implementation also exists; this page and its privacy policy describe the Apple mobile/companion and Android implementations, not the Windows application.
