Scout

Write and organize reports with local phrase completion, structured controls and optional AI assistance.

Scout combines a text workspace with shortcuts, corpus-based suggestions and configurable structured reports. Dictation and AI tools can extend the workflow, while provider and indexing status show which services are ready.

Description reviewed November 2, 2026

Setup & support Read the privacy policy

Local writing tools

Use shortcuts and prefix suggestions built from your own corpus without requiring a language-model call for each keystroke.

Structured reports

Create and edit report types, sections and controls manually, or review proposals produced by the optional AI workflow.

Dictation choices

Use a local Whisper installation or a configured remote speech service, with optional language-model refinement of the transcript.

Separate AI services

Configure a language-model provider and an embeddings service independently for completion, chat and contextual retrieval.

Desktop and document-editor integrations

The Tauri desktop app is separate from the OnlyOffice plugin and the Word add-in. Their document access, settings and deployment requirements differ; this page and policy describe the desktop application.
