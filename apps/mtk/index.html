MTK — Metal Toolkit

Render medical volumes, multiplanar views, and projections using Metal.

MTK is a rendering core for developers building Apple imaging applications. It accepts decoded volumes and produces GPU textures that your app can display or explicitly export.

Setup & support Read the privacy policy

Render on the GPU

Build volume rendering, multiplanar reconstruction, and projection views from shared Metal volume resources.

Shape the display

Control transfer functions, window settings, and volume layers for the visualization your application needs.

Bring your own application

The core package provides rendering components and synthetic fixtures. Your application supplies DICOM decoding, interface controls, and its data policies.
