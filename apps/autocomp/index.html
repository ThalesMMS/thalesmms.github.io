AutoComp for Safari

Your next words, right in Google Docs.

Get inline AI suggestions as you write. Choose an OpenAI-compatible HTTPS endpoint, connect your model, and keep working in your document.

Setup & supportHow your data is used

Stay in the document

Suggestions appear beside the cursor in editable Google Docs documents in Safari.

Accept at your pace

Press Tab for the next word, Shift + Tab for the full suggestion, or Esc to dismiss it.

Choose your endpoint

Enter your API base URL, model, and API key. AutoComp sends text context to that endpoint to request a suggestion.

Before you begin

AutoComp targets Safari 18 or later on macOS 15 or later. An AI provider, API credit, or hosted account is not included. Your chosen endpoint must support the OpenAI-compatible API over HTTPS.

Google Docs changes can affect extension behavior. See support and troubleshooting for help.
