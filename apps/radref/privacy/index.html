RadRef / Privacy

RadRef privacy policy

Effective September 5, 2026

How RadRef handles data, including the services and integrations you choose to use.

Clinical inputs

Calculations run locally. The inspected application does not implement a patient registry, clinical database or user account system; avoid adding identifying information to text you share.

Stored preferences

Language selection and favorite tool identifiers are stored locally. Clinical form inputs are handled in the current interface rather than saved as patient records.

Sharing

Copy and share actions place generated report text on the clipboard or pass it to the application you choose. That destination controls its own handling of the text.

External references

Opening a publication or professional-society link contacts its website. Calculations themselves do not call a backend or AI provider.

Analytics and deletion

No integrated analytics or crash-reporting service was found in the inspected application source. Clear application or browser site data to reset stored preferences; remove copied or shared material separately.

This support website

This static website does not use analytics scripts, forms, or third-party tracking scripts. GitHub Pages handles requests to the website under its own hosting and privacy practices. Visiting a source repository or contacting another service is subject to that service's policies.

Privacy questions and support

Contact Thales Matheus M Santos about RadRef at thalesmmsradio@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please omit patient information, private documents, and credentials.

Policy updates

This policy may change when the software's data handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version. Features and integrations can differ by release and platform.
